    How This Works

    Spotify deprecated its per-track "audio features" (energy, valence, tempo, etc.) for new apps in late 2024, so this doesn't read pre-computed audio DNA. Instead, each genre in your library maps to a hand-built instrument archetype, weighted by how often that genre shows up in your top artists; a sustained pad plays one of five hand-written chord progressions in your session's key, deterministically picked so it's consistently "yours"; and your single most-listened-to genre picks a matching synthesized drum pattern, tempo-matched to the session's BPM. Tempo and mode are fixed to a consistent, pleasant range regardless of your library — only the instrumentation, layering, harmony, and rhythm reflect your actual taste. The mix continuously adds, removes, and mutates layers for as long as you leave the tab open; Shuffle changes the musical mode (and, in turn, the chord progression) and toggles the drums to their other pattern, both landing cleanly on the next bar — everything else stays put.
